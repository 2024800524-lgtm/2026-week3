3rd Week: Lets bake Chocolate cookies

Ingredients

  • Unsalted butter
  • Granulated sugar
  • Eggs
  • Vanilla
  • All-purpose flour
  • Unsweetened cocoa powder
  • Baking soda
  • Salt
  • Flaky salt or coarse sugar, for sprinkling

Steps

    1. Cream butter and sugar
    2. Add eggs and vanilla
    3. Add dry ingredients
    4. Portion and bake
